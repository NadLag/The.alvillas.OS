# Configured recurring expense drafts

F-FIN-26 requires recurring expenses and payouts without inventing actual invoices or moving money before their existing approval and payment workflows.

The `finance.recurring_expenses` rule explicitly supplies schedules, exact decimal amounts, property and funding sources, civil send time, month-end behavior and batch size. The current configuration author must retain configuration and actual expense/funding authority. No system administrator or role name implicitly grants financial access. The scheduler reuses `createExpenseDraft` and its validators; its final authority fence rolls back the batch after revocation.

The existing signed dispatcher polls for work. A tenant/configuration lock serializes generation, and the append-only `recurring_expense_occurrence` table binds each original schedule/date to its actual draft, actor and immutable configuration version. Database constraints enforce tenant identity, unique dates, original draft facts and the configured recurrence. Missed dates resume in bounded batches. A changed configuration cannot replace completed history or produce the same schedule/date twice. Civil dates use the existing Temporal and IANA infrastructure. A repeated wall-clock time has one occurrence; a skipped wall-clock minute becomes due when that civil day's clock next passes its configured time.

The general configuration catalogue, previews and lifecycle commands apply the same independent expense and financial source permissions. Reads and writes retain caller restrictions and reread current authority after accessing or changing the policy. Configuration audit JSON, free-text reasons and CSV exports remain masked without the corresponding complete financial view/export grants. A configuration permission or presentation profile alone cannot expose template amounts or funding identities.

Generated drafts preserve the existing submission, approval, period, posting, void and exact opposite correction workflows. They create no synthetic supplier invoice or financial transaction. Recurring company expenses and owner payouts require their actual independent company and payout sources; a company expense is never attributed to a fictitious property. PR #130 remains draft pending its authenticated staging acceptance. Those requirements, localized controls and complete release acceptance remain required before F-FIN-26 can be DONE.
