# ADR0203: reviewed original signature identity recovery

Creation can take effect even when its acknowledgement is lost. An authenticated callback can be retained before creation identity is known, but cannot by itself authorize adoption of a provider request. Repeating creation risks a second legal/business request.

An independently authorized operator selects a retained native callback, reviews an account-scoped provider readback against the complete original PDF, recipient contract and captured contacts, then admits a separate durable recovery command. Inputs contain native signature and inbox references, reason, review fingerprint and private actor nonce. Provider IDs, URLs, tenant scope and outcomes are never caller-selected. Current and held source, template, financial, signature-edit, counterparty and field authority are checked before review/admission and after their audits. Another operator uses their own actual authority after requester offboarding. Original configuration and contacts remain the comparison contract; recovery performs no new upload or distribution.

Identity fingerprints bind the account, actual request/reference, original contract/PDF and provider creation date when supplied. Mutable signing progress is excluded so legitimate progress between review and execution cannot substitute identity. The worker repeats actual account/envelope/original-PDF GET verification, rechecks original operator authority, and records encrypted proof on the existing original PREPARE attempt. A separately committed recovery command and atomic system audit are mandatory. Forced tenant isolation, immutable history, shared document-protocol nonce protection and native identity-conflict guards remain mandatory.

A readback-confirmed private DRAFT/NONE is PREPARED with explicit READBACK_IDENTITY_RECOVERY provenance and a NULL creation receipt. An already advanced request retains UNCERTAIN evidence with SIGNATURE_PRIVATE_PREPARATION_ADVANCED and its actual readback; it never pretends to be a private draft or creation acknowledgement. Distribution of a recovered private draft independently references that retained preparation proof. Existing version1 ciphertext/AAD and acknowledged creation receipts remain compatible.

After identity commits, the existing authenticated callback worker verifies its retained notification through GETs. Interrupted callback verification keeps the human recovery receipt UNCERTAIN and recoverable. Redelivery can complete independent historical observations after operator deactivation without disclosing a private human response. Provider completion never becomes native SIGNED until F-DOC-07 archives and verifies the signed PDF and required certificate. No cancellation, expiry or uncertain provider outcome releases financial claims or rewrites financial sources.

Forward199 adds one append-only tenant recovery table and a nullable provenance FK on existing provider evidence; the 198 prior migration records/checksums and every prior column/tuple version are retained. Its SQL is frozen after local application. No production migration, legal approval, hosted provider acceptance or Vercel restriction bypass is implied.
