# 0161 — Source-bound service proceeds allocation

The service financial flow allocates original proceeds into owner, company, provider and tax entitlement. Cash collection establishes custody and unallocated ownership first; allocation cannot create another receipt or change the physical location of funds. Completed service evidence, native collection identity and the original policy snapshot must bind each admission. Later service orders must reference this original financial source rather than fabricate a second one.

Allocation rules belong in versioned configuration. Each explicit recipe states its service, currency, fixed obligations, proportions of proceeds remaining after those obligations, remainder beneficiary and rounding priority. Fixed obligations cannot exceed actual proceeds. Apportion the remaining minor units by largest fractional remainder, with the configured priority resolving exact ties. Preserve every cent and validate PostgreSQL monetary limits without JavaScript monetary floats. A company portion represents its configured entitlement before any separately recognized costs; an estimated internal cost cannot create a duplicate provider payable.

Tax assessment remains a separate policy interface. Every recipe identifies the tax decisions it requires, and every allocation must match an independent assessment for each decision, including an explicitly approved zero assessment. Allocation chooses no legal rate, exemption or accounting treatment. Missing policy or assessment denies admission.

Provider entitlements must preserve their actual supplier/person identity and join the canonical payable source and payment history. Owner portions must retain the original property ownership evidence. Financial commands must intersect held and current source authority, use native journal, period and lock helpers, commit source acknowledgement and audit atomically, and correct history through exact opposite entries. Estimates, incomplete service records and unverified tax assessments cannot authorize postings.

Provider accounting treatment is an explicit configuration decision, with no default. Under the supported `REVENUE_WITH_EXISTING_COMPANY_COST` recipe, an original company-funded invoice or employee cost already recognized the provider obligation, and its native expense/payable journal remains authoritative. Service allocation credits company service revenue for both the company portion and that already recognized provider cost, while retaining the actual provider source identity. The original expense reduces company net position; allocation creates no second expense or payable. A 500 MAD collection with 100 owner entitlement, 300 recognized provider cost and 100 company portion therefore leaves location 500, owner payable 100, provider payable 300 and company net 100. Whole-invoice and employee-claim payments keep their native scope. Missing or different accounting treatment is denied; other approved treatments require their own policy adapter.
