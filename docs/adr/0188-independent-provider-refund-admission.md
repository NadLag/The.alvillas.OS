# Independent admission of an already-paid provider refund

A committed external refund remains a financial fact after its initiating account loses access. Keep its original requester, request nonce, reason, source, policy, provider evidence and settlement immutable. An independently authorized financial reviewer may record that fact under their own identity; they never impersonate the original requester or issue another provider payment.

Require current financial view, override, reconciliation, creation and payment grants on the original source and economic date, with separate merchant-account visibility and payment authority. A committed reasoned review freezes that reviewer's authorization cap and source fingerprint. Intersect it with the submitted restrictions and current stored grants during admission and after the final audit. Ordinary expense permissions and approval remain separate requirements for any returned cost.

Reuse the canonical guest-refund producer with the original request nonce, exact Money and first independently verified provider completion date. A process-local authority binds the native producer to the actual tenant transaction, reviewer and original intent. It cannot be constructed by an HTTP payload. Its final-witness callback runs after the canonical refund audit and before the existing mandatory native guards. The canonical audit preserves the original refund reason; a separate supervised audit records both people and the reviewer's reason and command nonce.

Native constraints permit a distinct journal writer only when the independent review was committed earlier and authorization, final witness and canonical journal share one transaction. Require the original capture, refund, source, first completion and committed settlement. Preserve every existing source, budget, period, balance and immutability guard. A dangling authorization or missing final witness cannot commit. Applied SQL stays frozen; changes use additive migrations.

Recovery uses the reviewer's new nonce and the original canonical journal. It checks current and held financial visibility, returns that journal once and creates no money movement. Refunds with a returned fee remain rejected until their independent ordinary cost-correction and approval handoff is qualified.
