# 0186 — Original provider refund reservations

A provider refund is a separate physical money movement against an admitted original capture. It cannot be represented by reversing the capture receipt or by an unattested merchant debit. Keep the original guest claim, merchant location, currency, actor and command identity through the existing guest deposit producer.

Before provider I/O, commit an immutable refund intent, held authority and outbox reference. Reserve its exact amount against the original deposit's derived remaining liability under the same source lock used by ordinary refunds and accommodation applications. A reservation is metadata; every balance still comes from ledger movements. An additive PostgreSQL guard fences every affected claim posting independently of application checks.

Commit one attempt before the monetary POST. An unknown acknowledgement leaves the original funds reserved and never causes another POST. A known provider refund identity is reconciled by authenticated GET. Explicit reconciliation of an unknown identity must verify the original capture, request identity, amount and currency before retaining any fact. Provider links are validated evidence and are never followed with credentials.

Keep provider identities globally unique and retain observations append-only, with compound tenant references, forced RLS and attributed system audits. Provider observations may be retained after current financial grants end; they confer no authority to admit a journal. Pending, failed and unknown outcomes retain their reservation until an explicit safe release proves that no monetary effect can still occur. Financial admission requires previously committed completed evidence, the original producer and independent held/current authority. Authoritative physical refunds require their own source corrections rather than manual bookkeeping opposites.

The [PayPal refund endpoint](https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments/v2/captures-refund) supports explicit partial amounts, request identity and caller correlation. The [refund status endpoint](https://developer.paypal.com/api/payments/v2/refunds-get) supplies read-only reconciliation. Neither an absent response nor an absent settlement breakdown establishes that no funds moved or that fees are zero. Original gross, returned fees, merchant net and provider economic time must be verified before financial admission.
