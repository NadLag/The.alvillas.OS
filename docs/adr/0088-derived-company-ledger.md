# 0088 — Derived ALVILLAS company ledger

F-FIN-19 reads existing `ALVILLAS_OWN` posting accounts. Its net position is credits minus debits: commission and service revenue increase it; company expenses reduce it. Owner liabilities, supplier/employee liabilities, physical bank/cash/wallet balances and suspense remain in their existing domains. The reader makes no tax, statutory-accounting or payroll assumption and creates no financial rows or stored balances. Corrections appear through the existing append-only reversal/adjustment postings, including archived history.

The independent `company_ledger:view` permission is required in both current stored authority and the caller context. Conditional or property/owner/record-limited grants cannot authorize an organization aggregate. Standard admin, finance and accountant templates include company access; owner and operational templates do not. Existing catalog reconciliation provisions the new permission. The shared `authorize` evaluator and `present` field policies remain authoritative. DTOs omit posting dimensions, transaction descriptions, counterpart accounts and personal compensation metadata.

One tenant-scoped Repeatable Read transaction uses exact PostgreSQL numeric aggregates and `Money` totals. Two fixed posting queries provide per-account totals and a 50-row history page; a database window computes running balances before pagination, preserving microsecond ordering and cents. Existing tenant/account posting and transaction indexes serve these queries. Civil-date boundaries, current IANA timezone checks, configuration, audit writer and session handling are shared with existing finance readers. Staging remains a required acceptance gate.
