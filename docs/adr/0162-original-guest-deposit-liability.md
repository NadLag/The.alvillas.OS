# 0162 — Original guest deposit liability and refunds

An actual guest deposit debits its configured physical receipt account and credits a separate guest liability. It creates no owner entitlement, commission or company revenue. The original reservation, property, guest, currency, receipt and selected policy remain attributable in immutable journal history. The handbook's `GUEST_PREPAYMENT` recipe supplies the accommodation prepayment interface. Other explicitly configured refundable deposit kinds use their own guest liability; a refund-only kind cannot be treated as accommodation earnings.

Use the existing financial transaction, sealed postings, idempotency and atomic audit writer for original receipts, partial refunds and exact opposite corrections. Do not introduce another ledger or a stored balance. Derive each original deposit's remaining liability from its own posted movements and opposite corrections. A different guest's funds or the total physical bank balance cannot enlarge that amount. A refund consumes only the unapplied, unrefunded original liability, in its original currency, through an explicitly configured payment route.

Recognition against accommodation remains a separate native financial operation. It must consume the original liability and use the reservation's original accounting and commission decisions. Refunding an already recognized stay continues through its native reservation correction flow; a cash refund must not silently reverse unrelated earnings or a previously paid provider. Do not default forfeiture, tax treatment, refund deadlines, commission changes or other legal/accounting choices. Unsupported treatment fails closed behind the explicit policy interface.

Held and current financial authority apply independently of operational reservation access. Review and durable commands retain their actual actor, full intent, policy version and source fingerprint. Source and account locks, accounting-period guards, tenant boundaries and final authorization checks protect the same transaction as the journal and audit. Closed-period corrections append their exact opposite in the current open period while preserving every original row.

The source audit for an opposite also carries the journal engine's original/reversal ID lists. Its shared constraint verifies the complete opposite vector and atomic audit; the guest-source constraint separately protects original liability, route identity and nonnegative consumption. A later deposit-kind change does not replace an earlier receipt's liability. Refund routes and methods are checked against the current selected rule through the existing reservation configuration scope resolver, including booking-source/property precedence.
