# 0286. Maintenance estimates and native financial records

Accepted for F-MNT-02 and F-MNT-03.

A work-order estimate is an immutable revision of the registered Operations task. The property-cost policy resolves allocation and approval thresholds. Its configuration, allocation, ordered stages, and recipients are captured at publication. Required stages use the shared approval engine and notification inbox. A pending review resolves before a further estimate revision can be recorded.

Maintenance links retain the exact version and amount of an authorized native expense or property-scoped supplier invoice. Finance owns invoice recognition, expense submission, journal posting, and corrections. Publishing an estimate records the forecast and its approval history. A pending or rejected estimate prevents native work start or completion, and linked expenses require completed work before posting.

Tenant policies, immutable records, audit retention, native source checks, and replay protection apply to both work-order finance and the linked source.
