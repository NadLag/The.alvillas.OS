# Independent company expense plans and civil recurrence

F-FIN-26 requires scheduled company costs; F-FIN-27 keeps company expenses separate from property expenses. An estimated scheduled cost cannot become a supplier invoice before an actual invoice exists.

Company expense identities and their consecutive revisions are append-only tenant records. A revision captures exact Money, the configured category and cost center, organization calendar and original policy snapshot. Cancellation appends a new revision while preserving all source facts. It does not delete a source or post a reversal. Financial and payroll permissions are independent; a cost that has ever carried salary classification keeps that protection through every later revision.

Configured recurrence uses the same anchored civil calendar as property recurrence. The current configuration author must hold current organization authority. Tenant locks, unique schedule/date identities and source/audit database guards admit one original plan per due civil occurrence. Bounded catch-up preserves missed dates. Final authorization checks roll back the whole batch after revocation. Jobs create plans; approval, actual invoices, recognition and actual payment remain subject to the canonical source workflows.

The general configuration catalog and audit surfaces protect recurring company templates through independent company-expense, financial-transaction and payroll grants. Company plans have no property attribution. Neither planned amounts nor a schedule imply an actual accounting movement. Linking actual company invoice/compensation sources, payment tracking and the complete localized workspace remain required before F-FIN-27 can be DONE. No legal, tax or accounting policy is inferred.
