# 0090 — Supplier claims use separate recognition and settlement journals

Supplier invoice recognition follows handbook G.3: debit the configured owner, company or guest allocations and credit `SUPPLIER_PAYABLE` with the supplier, invoice and allocation identities. It does not credit a physical funding account. Each line reuses the existing expense posting recipe and its conservation checks; configured owner amounts and agreement snapshots remain the source of the allocation. Company lines can carry a cost center without a fictional property or owner. Guest amounts require a real reservation/property source.

Settlement debits the existing supplier claim and credits an eligible bank, cash or staff-wallet location. It never books the expense again. Outstanding money is the exact sum of claim postings, including reversals, filtered by organization, supplier, invoice and currency. There is no editable balance. All settlements and reversals for an invoice must acquire the same tenant-scoped transaction lock before reading its balance; concurrent requests cannot both spend the same outstanding amount. The existing financial writer supplies period enforcement, atomic journals and immutable history.

The posting recipes and private repository are internal dependencies of F-FIN-23. Public invoice commands must additionally require current caller/stored financial authority, validate every source in its tenant, retain idempotent request identity, freeze configured allocations and approval stages, and record attributed audit in the posting transaction. Invoice authoring, approval integration, immutable source records, payment commands and native acceptance must be complete before exposing that workflow or marking F-FIN-23 DONE. This decision does not create a second approval, authorization, money or ledger implementation.
