# Complete supplier claim reversal

An approved supplier invoice retains its immutable recognition and every original cost journal. Reversal appends the exact opposite of every original journal in one transaction, using the existing batched Money writer and attributed financial-reversal receipt. Every actual supplier payment must first have its own exact reversal. The outstanding claim is derived from movements and must be zero after the complete reversal. New payments cannot settle a reversed claim.

Original caller restrictions and current stored grants protect the whole invoice, original economic and posting dates, every contributing payment and funding wallet, and creation in the current open accounting period. The common source fence also rejects a property city or region changing during an operation, so an earlier captured scope cannot authorize a later write. This is tested through a genuine funding-lock wait and property-city change.

Same-actor replay requires the original reason, source version and nonce. It returns reversal identities in original cost order. Different intent conflicts after financial access checks. Original allocation decisions, owner shares, payment receipts, journals and audits remain unchanged. Expiry after actual deferred guards rolls back every new reversal and its receipt.

Forward migration 79 shares the invoice advisory lock with payments before raw reversal postings. Deferred guards retain the actual original source and require a complete cost set, one canonical attributed receipt and zero outstanding in every contributing currency. The smallest actual attempted reversal drives the complete-set check, so a partial attempted set cannot avoid it. Existing global exact-opposite, audit and open-period guards still apply. All previous applied SQL bytes stay frozen.

Actual reversal of 500 original costs takes 8.2 seconds, creates 500 opposite journals and 1,000 postings, and retains all original histories and replay order. Execution therefore belongs behind the durable financial request worker before browser controls are exposed. This kernel adds no editable balance, replacement expense or external payment-provider action.
