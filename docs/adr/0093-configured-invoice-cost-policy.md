# 0093 — Invoice policy uses actual cost sources and explicit company revenue

Unpaid supplier invoices reuse the existing configured expense allocation and approval engine. The property-cost preparation function accepts actual invoice-line identities without creating an expense, fictitious property, owner or physical funding account. The paid-expense wrapper retains its existing snapshot contract and correction behavior. Property and reservation costs retain the same ownership shares, original booking agreement evidence, configured treatments, reference rules, calendar windows and emergency owner-notice requirements.

An invoice supplies an explicit economic date and emergency flag. Its vendor issue and due dates stay unchanged. Every line retains its own property calendar or the configured organization calendar. Approval thresholds evaluate the entire invoice amount, preventing subdivision into lines from waiving configured review. Each resulting stage identifies its actual line and property/reservation source. Recipient authorization and stage amounts use that owner's exact share of the line; other owners' charges and unrelated company lines are absent from that stage's amount. Full snapshots remain restricted to invoice editors with the financial authority for every source.

Company lines resolve organization configuration through the same allocation/approval evaluator. Owner or guest allocations and owner routing fail closed when no property/owner source exists. A company percentage threshold requires `revenue_source.basis = COMPANY_ACCOUNTS` and an explicit distinct list of `account_codes` in `finance.approval_thresholds`, alongside the existing revenue period. Eligible accounts must belong to the organization and be `ALVILLAS_OWN` with a credit normal side. The reader sums exact signed movements, including reversals, in the configured currency and calendar. It excludes custody, claims and expense accounts. Property costs retain their existing owner-gross revenue basis, optionally expressed as `OWNER_GROSS`; a company basis cannot silently replace it.

Planning requires both current and caller invoice/supplier authority and financial authority for every source. Whole-property policies cannot be read through a single-record grant. Company revenue additionally requires explicit organization company-ledger access before any revenue query. Source permissions are refreshed again after preparation. The planner returns evidence and proposed stages without creating a journal, expense or approval. Durable submission, shared approval adapters, liability recognition and settlement/reversal remain separate required transitions before F-FIN-23 can be complete.

Configured role conditions can either waive review or add it. Intersecting role names could therefore remove a mandatory review. Invoice preparation instead requires caller and current policy-role contexts to agree, captures the actual role names per line and checks them again after preparation. Role drift requires an authorization refresh before planning can proceed. Regressions cover both waiver and obligation conditions, including revocation during the actual configuration read; a refreshed context produces the configured required review.

Real PostgreSQL coverage includes mixed company/property costs, missing calendars, denied or revoked sources, original booking agreements, ordered independently scoped co-owner stages, 500 actual property allocations, configured company income, currency/calendar/tenant isolation and exact reversing movements. Existing paid-expense and company-ledger regressions remain required.
