# 0163 — Guest ownership from an original field cash collection

A field collection already records physical custody and an unallocated ownership claim. Guest deposit admission reclassifies only the original unallocated claim into the configured guest liability. It creates no second collection, physical movement, owner earning, commission or payment event. Each admission retains its original collection, custody account, task provenance, booking guest at collection time, currency and selected policy. The optional cash-admission policy has no business defaults.

All consumers of an original collection share its existing ledger movements and a source lock. An additive deferred PostgreSQL guard enforces the original same-tenant receipt budget for legacy camel-case and native snake-case collection identities. This also prevents the original physical collection's opposite while a guest or service ownership allocation remains active. Existing source-admission guards run first and retain their independently tested rejection boundaries. Applied migrations remain unchanged.

The original guest journal and its atomic audit provide admission durability. The existing guest-source reader, nonce recovery, refund routes and exact current-period opposite handle cash-backed claims as well as direct bank receipts. Cash-source visibility and payment authority remain independent of operational access. Private bounded source selection, original-custody disclosure and command recovery repeat held and current authority, including after audit writes. Every financial balance remains derived from immutable postings.

The configured closed-period rejection applies to the original collection date as well as the new admission date. A first admission cannot move a closed source into a later open month. The existing accounting-period helper and shared calendar lock enforce this in both the service and an additive SQL guard. Exact opposites of previously admitted claims use the current open period and retain the original closing snapshot.
