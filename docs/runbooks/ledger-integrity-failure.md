# Ledger integrity failure

Open the authenticated audit destination from the original failure notification. Confirm its organization, retained run ID, policy version, checked time and failure codes. Review the retained report and original journal references using independently authorized financial access. The checker never repairs balances or changes financial history.

For a failed comparison, preserve the original report, journals, postings, source fingerprints and relevant deployment/CI evidence. Stop dependent financial operations through the existing authorized operational controls when the observed failure affects their correctness. Investigate the reported account, transaction, currency, tenant dimension, closed-period snapshot or native history guard. Do not disable constraints, edit balances, rewrite applied SQL, or delete/rewrite financial rows. Any accounting correction or financial-history change requires the owner decision described in the handbook.

If the dispatcher reports `authority_unavailable`, restore the configured principal's approved native grants and MFA enrollment through normal identity administration. For `configuration_unavailable`, review the organization-level `finance.ledger_integrity`, accounting calendar and `notification.routing.ledger_integrity_failed` rules. Failure routing requires explicit eligible people or roles and channels. It grants no financial access. Review original/current versions before saving a replacement through the configuration writer.

For `source_changed`, inspect whether configuration or original journals changed during the coherent scan; a later signed dispatcher tick can try again without publishing an incomplete result. Unexpected infrastructure errors remain failed background jobs and use the existing operational alerting. Inspect durable outbox and delivery receipts for channel failures; use existing recovery procedures rather than creating a second source event or sending duplicate notices. Native per-day admission and source-bound delivery preserve idempotency.

After the cause is resolved, verify a passing retained occurrence at the configured local time, its audit record and the owner's authenticated destination. A first fingerprint capture establishes a baseline at that moment; it cannot prove an earlier financial state. Production migration/promotion and legal or accounting decisions remain owner-reserved.
